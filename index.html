Our Child Development Oriented Program

Five dimensions, nurtured every single day.

Cognitive

Puzzles, guided discovery and everyday problem-solving build memory, reasoning and early thinking at each child's own pace.

  • Storytelling & vocabulary
  • Early maths & shapes
  • Focus & memory games

Physical

Outdoor play in a large play area, movement games and fine motor work build coordination, strength and body confidence.

  • Motor skills & yoga
  • Health & nutrition
  • Sensory exploration

Emotional

A secure, nurturing environment helps children name their feelings, build empathy and grow self-regulation.

  • Confidence & affirmations
  • Expressing emotions
  • Empathy & respect

Creative

Art, music, pretend play and open-ended exploration give every child room to imagine and express.

  • Arts, crafts & imagination
  • Drama & role-play
  • Singing & painting

Cultural, social & moral

Stories, festivals and group play teach sharing, values and respect for others from an early age.

  • Cultures & traditions
  • Empathy & kindness
  • Honesty & responsibility
